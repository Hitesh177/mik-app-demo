Looking for the best place near 101 to catch Taipei’s stunning skyline? Xiangshan, also known as Elephant Mountain, is a must-visit spot when you travel to Taiwan. Nestled in the southeastern part of Taipei, this iconic hiking trail offers breathtaking city views and has become a favorite destination for both locals and travelers. From beginners to seasoned hikers, everyone can enjoy the beauty of Taipei from above.

MRT Red Line - Xiangshan

The hike up Xiangshan is surprisingly easy and accessible. Located just a short walk from the MRT Red Line, it’s perfect for travelers who want a quick escape from the bustling city. The trail consists of well-maintained stairs and several scenic rest points, making it easy to pause, take photos, or simply soak in the surrounding greenery. As you ascend, the city gradually spreads out below you, with the towering Taipei 101 creating a spectacular backdrop for every shot.

One of the best parts? The area around Xiangshan is dotted with cozy cafes and small eateries. You can grab a coffee and relax before your hike, or celebrate reaching the top with a treat while enjoying the view. This combination of urban convenience and natural beauty makes Xiangshan a unique experience—perfect for both adventure seekers and those who love a more leisurely exploration.

Sunset at Xiangshan is truly magical. As the warm golden light bathes the city, the streets seem to slow down, and Taipei’s skyline glows with a mesmerizing charm. Photographers, influencers, and casual travelers alike flock here to capture the moment. Whether you’re traveling solo, with friends, or on a family trip, a hike up Xiangshan transforms into a memorable adventure you won’t forget.

If you’re planning to travel to Taiwan, don’t miss Xiangshan. It’s one of the most accessible viewpoints in Taipei, offering the perfect blend of nature, cityscape, and unforgettable views. Conveniently located near the MRT Red Line and surrounded by cozy cafes, it’s easy to spend a full day hiking, relaxing, and soaking in Taipei’s beauty from every angle. Truly, Xiangshan is a must-visit place in Taiwan for anyone seeking a complete Taipei experience.

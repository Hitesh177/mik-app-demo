🌿 Why Fresh Paneer is a Must in Your Kitchen

Paneer, often called Indian cottage cheese, is a staple in Indian cooking – and making it fresh changes everything.

✨ Pure & Nutritious

Fresh paneer is soft, rich in protein, packed with calcium, and free from preservatives. It’s excellent for growing children, working adults, and even the elderly.

🍛 Boosts Curry Flavour

Unlike store-bought versions, fresh paneer soaks up spices and gravy like a sponge – giving your curry an unbelievably creamy, authentic taste.

🧑‍🍳 Easy Step-by-Step Recipe to Make Paneer at Home

We’re excited to share our MIK secret method to create soft, tasty paneer in your own kitchen.

Step 1️⃣: Prepare the Milk

Take 1 litre of full-fat fresh milk 🥛. You may optionally mix in a spoonful of whole milk powder for richness. Pour into a clean saucepan.

Step 2️⃣: Mix Smooth

If using milk powder, blend the milk with a beater until smooth. Add a little water if needed 💧.

Step 3️⃣: Bring to Boil

Heat the milk, stirring continuously to avoid sticking or burning 🔥🌀.

Step 4️⃣: Curdle the Milk

Add lemon juice 🍋 or vinegar to the hot milk to curdle it. Watch as soft white curds separate from the whey.

Pro Tip: Add chopped ginger, methi leaves, or fresh coriander 🌿 to infuse natural flavour into your paneer.

Step 5️⃣: Strain It

Use a clean muslin cloth or fine sieve to separate the curds. Let it cool, then squeeze out all excess water 🧺❄️.

Step 6️⃣: Chill and Set

Place the curds in the fridge to firm up. Once set, cut the block into neat cubes 🔲.

Step 7️⃣: Cook Something Amazing!

Now that you’ve made fresh paneer, use it in your favourite dish – we highly recommend trying our Paneer Tikka Masala recipe! 🍛❤️

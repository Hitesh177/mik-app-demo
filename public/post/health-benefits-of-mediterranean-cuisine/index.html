Fresh and Nutrient:

It use of fresh fruits and vegetables, whole grains, legumes, nuts, seeds, which are rich source of essential nutrients, including vitamins, minerals, fiber, etc.

Heart-Healthy Fats: Olive oil is a key component of Mediterranean cooking and is a healthier alternative. It contains monounsaturated fats, which have been linked to improved heart health by reducing bad cholesterol levels.

Health Benefits: Numerous studies have linked the Mediterranean diet to various health benefits. It has been associated with a reduced risk of heart disease, lower blood pressure, improved blood sugar control, decreased inflammation, and a lower risk of obesity.

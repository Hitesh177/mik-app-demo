Shipping & Delivery Across Taiwan

How Long Does Delivery Take?

Delivery time depends on your location and the total weight of your order.

Same-Day Cash on Delivery (COD)

Available in: Taipei City, Taoyuan City, Hsinchu, Zhubei, and Taichung City Delivery methods include Lalamove or private delivery partners.

  • Within 3 km of an MIK Store branch: 30–45 minutes

  • Within 5–10 km: Approximately 90 minutes or more

  • Orders must be placed before 2:00 PM for same-day delivery

Nationwide Delivery (7-11, FamilyMart, or Courier Services)

Available throughout Taiwan with an estimated delivery time of 24–72 hours:

  • Taoyuan, Taichung: 1 business day

  • Tainan, Kaohsiung: 1–2 business days

  • Hualien, Yilan: 2–3 business days

Remote or mountainous areas may require additional delivery time. Weather conditions and public holidays may also affect delivery schedules.

Common Reasons for Delivery Delays

To ensure smooth delivery, please note the following common issues:

  1. Incorrect or incomplete address details (Chinese addresses are strongly preferred)

  2. Incorrect location pin or inactive phone number

  3. Most delivery personnel are Taiwanese and primarily speak Mandarin

If you do not speak Mandarin, arranging assistance from a local friend is highly recommended.

Order Tracking

After your order is confirmed, you will receive:

  1. An automated order confirmation email

  2. A phone call from the MIK Store team (orders are processed on a first-come, first-served basis)

To check the delivery status:

  • Contact MIK Store via LINE or phone

  • A QR code for tracking will be provided

Minimum Order & Delivery Fees

  • No minimum order requirement

  • Free delivery:

    • Orders above NT$1,000 within a 5 km radius of MIK Store branches in Taipei, Taoyuan, Zhubei, and Taichung

    • Orders above NT$3,000 across Taiwan (up to 10 kg)

Additional delivery charges:

  • Approximately NT$60 for every additional 5 kg, calculated by the courier company based on distance and weight

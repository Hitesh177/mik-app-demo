Core vegetarian staples

Vegetarian cooking depends on repeatable ingredients. Dal, rice, spices, ghee, and fresh leaves are the backbone of soups, curries, tiffin boxes, and comfort meals.

If you are cooking without meat, the quality of your flavor base matters even more. Good spice blends and fresh herbs make simple dishes taste richer and more satisfying.

How to keep vegetarian meals interesting

A vegetarian grocery basket gets boring only when everything tastes the same. The fix is to keep a few spices, fresh herbs, and ready-to-eat items around so weekday meals still feel varied.

Add one frozen bread product and one ready meal and suddenly your kitchen can handle rushed mornings, late nights, and weekend guests without much stress.

•Paratha Frozen 5pcs and Mini Naan Frozen 5pcs for quick bases.
•Biryani Masala, Garam Masala, and Chana Masala for flavor variety.
•Pav Bhaji or Rajma Masala when you need fast comfort food.

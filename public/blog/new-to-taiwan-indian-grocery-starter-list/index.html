Why this guide exists

If you are not sure where to start, begin with a mix of pantry items, a little freshness, and one or two convenience products. That combination gives you breathing room while you learn the local options.

Start with the essentials

The first few items in an Indian grocery basket are usually the most important: flour, dal, rice, ghee, oil, salt, and a couple of spice blends. These basics let you cook a wide range of meals without overbuying.

Once the kitchen is functional, add fresh herbs and convenience items like ready-to-eat meals or frozen breads. That keeps your grocery basket practical and your meals easy to manage.

•Atta Wheat Flour Aashirvaad 5kg.
•Moong Dal Yellow 907g and Toor/Arar Dal 907g.
•Pure Ghee Amul 1L and Mustard Oil 1L.

Add freshness and convenience

Fresh herbs and simple aromatics bring back the taste of home. Ready-to-eat meals and frozen breads make the transition easier when you are busy unpacking or working.

This mix also works well for people who do not yet know their final routine. It gives you flexibility while you learn what you actually use every week.

•Fresh herb packs and curry leaves for quick home-style cooking.
•Paratha Frozen 5pcs and Mini Naan Frozen 5pcs.
•Pav Bhaji MTR 300g or Lemon Rice MTR 300g for emergency meals.

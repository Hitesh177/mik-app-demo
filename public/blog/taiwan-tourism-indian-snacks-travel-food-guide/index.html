Why this guide exists

Whether you are visiting family, exploring Taiwan for a week, or picking up gifts, snacks and sweets are one of the easiest ways to bring familiar flavor into a new city.

What travel shoppers usually want

Travelers do not usually want a full pantry. They want small, easy-to-pack items that can be opened on the road, shared in a hotel room, or taken as a gift to friends and relatives.

This is where Indian snacks, papad, tea, and sweets do especially well. They are familiar, shelf-friendly, and easy to explain to someone receiving them for the first time.

•All In One Haldiram's 200g and Cheese Khakhra 200g for road snacks.

Best giftable Indian grocery items

A good travel gift should feel thoughtful and safe to carry. Sweets, biscuits, and small savory packs are ideal for that purpose because they are compact and easy to share.

If your tourist search is closer to “what can I eat while traveling?” then ready-to-eat meals and small snack packs are the most practical answer.

•Haldiram's snacks for familiar flavor.
•Papad and biscuits for sharing with hosts.
